# Code of conduct

These are fan projects about a kids' game series. Keep it friendly:

- Be kind and patient, also when something doesn't work. Most people here do this in their free
  time.
- No insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, comments or anywhere the
  projects are discussed.
- No piracy talk: no links to game files or ISOs, no asking for them.
- Stay on topic: issues are for problems and ideas around these projects.

Comments that break this are hidden or removed, and repeated or serious cases are blocked from the
repositories. If something bothers you, report it with GitHub's **Report content** (the ... menu on
a comment) or write in an issue.
